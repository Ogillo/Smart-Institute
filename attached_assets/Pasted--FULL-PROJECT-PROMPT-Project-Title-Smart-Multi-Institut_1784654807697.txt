# FULL PROJECT PROMPT

## Project Title

**Smart Multi-Institution Admission and Student Onboarding Management System**

---

# Project Overview

Build a modern, secure, responsive web-based Multi-Institution Admission and Student Onboarding Management System for Secondary Schools, Colleges, and Universities.

The system should digitalize the entire admission process from the time a student receives an admission/placement letter until the student reports to school and is officially registered.

The primary objective of the system is to reduce the physical reporting process to **less than 30 minutes**, especially for parents and guardians travelling from distant towns and counties.

The platform must support multiple institutions, each with its own administrators, admission requirements, fee structures, inventory, and student records.

---

The system should support the following user roles:

Responsible for managing the entire platform.

Permissions:

* Register and approve institutions
* Manage institution subscriptions
* Manage all users

Responsible for managing admissions within one institution.

Permissions:

* Approve admissions
* Allocate students
* Manage inventory
* View reports

## 3. Admission Officer

Permissions:

* Verify admission letters

## 6. Parent / Student

# PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The system should digitize and simplify the admission process across multiple institutions, allowing parents to complete nearly all admission requirements online before the reporting date. By handling document submission, fee payment, item purchases, and student allocation in advance, the system aims to reduce the reporting process to **less than 30 minutes**, improving efficiency for institutions and providing a convenient experience for parents and students, especially those travelling from distant locations.
